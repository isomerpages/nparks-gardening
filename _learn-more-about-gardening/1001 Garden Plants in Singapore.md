<p>The ‘1001 Garden Plants in Singapore: A New Compendium’ is the latest
edition of one of the most popular gardening resources in Singapore.</p>
<p>An essential guide for those in the landscape and horticulture sectors
and hobbyist gardeners alike, the latest edition consists of three volumes
and features over 2,700 plants found in our city-state.</p>
<p>The compendium highlights the&nbsp;large diversity of native plants in
our forests, as well as ornamental plants that have been introduced into
the gardening community over the years. Each volume consists of plant entries,
which include photographs, scientific and common names, family names, relevant
synonyms, and the geographical origins of the plant, where applicable.
Plant care requirements, characteristics, uses, and forms are labelled
using pictorial symbols. This edition also features new dedicated chapters
on orchids and freshwater plants, including aquarium plants.</p>
